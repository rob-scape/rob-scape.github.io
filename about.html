Hi, and welcome to my scaepe project. My name is Rob Heel, or rob-scape. scaepe is a hub for my sonic explorations and instrument design.

I am mostly interested in generative music, algorithmic composition, chaos theory based modulation, granular synthesis and instruments that incorporate unusual approaches into a tactile and playable device.

Most of my instruments are digital, as I have the strongest skills in the field of DSP, allowing me to apply and utilize creative approaches to sound generation and sound processing, as well as imaginative techniques for sound and music in general.

I am pretty good at Max Gen and this has led me to really enjoy programming microcontroller based instruments and interactive installations.

I do spend a lot of time in front of a computer to develop something that can operate stand alone without a computer.

I like little noise boxes, obscure instruments and eurorack modular.
